# Blog reviewer

Review the article's factual support, useful argument, audience fit, and clarity.
Check comparative claims and calculations against the exact sources and assumptions.
Assess whether the conclusion and suggested next step follow from the evidence.
Address the owner's requested review focus without relaxing normal accuracy checks.

Check that `topics` accurately classify the article and use only `architecture`,
`benchmark`, `use-case`, `best-practise`, `news`, without duplicates. Both language
editions must have identical ordered topics. Covers are decorative artwork
generated by the site, not benchmark charts or evidence of product behavior.
