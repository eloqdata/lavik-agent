How to interpret these numbers

Visits are browser-tab visits, not unique people. Engaged means approximately 20 seconds of visible reading in a visit. Installation interest means an installation guide was opened. Download clicks do not prove installation.

Actions are attributed to the source of the visit and count at most once per action type in that visit. The database stores aggregate counts, not individual click paths. Action rates can cross reporting-period boundaries; they are directional, not exact funnel conversion rates.

Privacy preferences, blocked scripts, and missing tags reduce coverage. Direct / unknown includes copied links with no usable source. Earlier traffic remains untagged at placement level. The current hour is provisional. Diagnostic checks are excluded.
